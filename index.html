Frequently asked questions

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. Whisper runs entirely on your Mac via whisper.cpp. After the first model download (75MB–1.5GB depending on quality tier), transcription works on a plane, in a tunnel, with WiFi off — anywhere.

How private is it?

Your voice and transcripts never leave your device. No cloud servers, no analytics, no account, no telemetry. You can verify by checking Activity Monitor's Network tab — Wavetype's outbound traffic stays at zero.

Mac App Store version vs Pro — which one?

The App Store version is sandboxed (Apple's rules), so it copies transcripts to your clipboard and you press Cmd+V. Pro adds auto-paste — text goes straight into the field your cursor is in. If you want the smoothest workflow, get Pro.

What about other languages?

Whisper supports 99+ languages and auto-detects what you're speaking. The app interface itself is localized in 14 languages.

How accurate is it?

Wavetype uses three Whisper model tiers: Ultra Fast (English-only, smallest), High (large-v3-turbo, the default — handles dictation, accents, and technical jargon well), and Maximum (full large-v3, slower, highest accuracy).

Refunds?

Mac App Store: handled by Apple. Pro: 30-day no-questions refund — email wavetype.app@gmail.com.
